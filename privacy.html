Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CD Industries ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Vertex Dungeon (the "App").

1. Information we directly collect

We do not require users to create an account, and CD Industries does not directly collect, store, sell, or maintain personal information about users through the App.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version of the App displays advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Although CD Industries does not directly collect personal information, Google and the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process information from or about your device in order to provide, measure, secure, limit, or personalize advertising, depending on your device settings, region, consent choices, and Google's practices.

Information processed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may include an IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), device or advertising identifiers, information about interactions with the App or advertisements, advertising data, crash or diagnostic information, and performance information.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

3. In-app purchase to remove ads

The App offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to remove advertisements. Purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple, as applicable.

CD Industries does not directly receive or store your full payment-card or banking information. Google or Apple may process transaction, account, device, and payment-related information according to their own privacy policies and store terms.

4. Data sharing

CD Industries does not sell personal information. Information may be processed or shared by Google in connection with AdMob advertising and by Google Play or Apple when needed for app distribution, purchase processing, fraud prevention, security, or related platform services. Those companies' handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because CD Industries does not directly maintain user accounts or personal information through the App, we generally do not hold personal data that can be deleted upon request.

Information processed independently by Google, Google Play, or Apple is subject to those providers' retention and deletion practices. Users may also be able to reset or limit advertising identifiers, personalized advertising, or tracking through their device and platform privacy settings.

6. Children's privacy

Vertex Dungeon is a general-audience application and is not directed to children under 13. The App does not require users to submit personal information directly to CD Industries. Advertising and related information processing are provided through Google AdMob as described above.

If we learn that the App's intended audience, features, or legal obligations have changed in a way that requires additional protections for children, we will update the App and this Privacy Policy as appropriate.

7. Security

CD Industries does not operate a server that stores personal information collected directly from users of the App. Third-party providers are responsible for the security measures applicable to information they process through their services.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including when the App's features, advertising services, or applicable legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
